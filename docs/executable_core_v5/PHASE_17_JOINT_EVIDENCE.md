## Formal composition and its remaining hypotheses

The new template composes `attachTaskSemantics` and `attachProvenance` on ONE
`Learned.FrontierKernel`. A theorem preserves frontier soundness through that
composition; a second exposes the bound proposal relation. A third states that
all six goal/resource/information value-and-budget functions remain identical to
the supplied compatible kernel. They are not replaced with zero or `True`.

`assemble_learned_step` constructs the ACTUAL root `Learned.LearnedAcceptedStep`
only when supplied with all remaining premises: concrete base `StepObligations`,
valid current context and produced/matched proposal, strict frontier expansion,
and the existing goal, resource and information inequalities. It protects the
entire inherited checked frontier rather than an arbitrary caller-selected subset.

This is a conditional assembly theorem, NOT an instantiation of a complete
accepted physical transition. No concrete compatible base/RCLM information model,
full goal interpretation, total resource witness or autonomous-history proof is
invented here. The observation functions remain parameters. The selected finite
certificate supplies facts about the measured fixture and shared identities;
physical-byte/observation/literal refinement is still an external trusted binding.

The complete source preserves existing proof bodies and hoists their imports.
It prints 43 exact audits: SIX new composition audits, 24 rechecked typed/frontier/
producer audits, and 13 inherited theorem propositions rechecked as task proof
arguments. These rechecks are mandatory on the same candidate; they do not turn
old reports into new runs. The 172 Phase 17H arithmetic-plan proofs are NOT rerun.
The operational runtime computations used by typed preparation ARE recomputed.

The compiler must accept the positive source and reject five separate controls:
`MixedCandidate`, `MixedTranscript`, `WrongComponent`, `WrongTarget`, and
`SkippedPrefix`. Missing tools, timeouts, incomplete proofs, unapproved axioms,
unexpected audit output and accepting any negative control all prevent success.

## Scope of a successful local result

A pass means same-candidate composition and the conditional assembler were
checked in their declared scope. It does not make a generic root theorem's
hypotheses true for a physical system. It does not close another historical
recursive task or make an external controller a self-hosted neural generator.
Hashing, canonical parsing, file/archival interpretation, score computation,
physical observations and Python-to-Lean literal bindings remain externally
checked but not universally formally verified. Toolchain/import-cache trust
assumptions remain explicit. The adapter is not independent process attestation.

The following remain false: `root_transition_verified`,
`recursive_productivity_recertified`, `full_frontier_recertified`,
`full_learned_semantic_refinement`, `neural_decoder_formally_verified`,
`complete_resource_accounting`, `promotion_authorized`, `promotion_performed`,
`live_capability_gain_demonstrated`, `phase17_exit_closed`, `gate_e_closed`,
and `egc0_started`. The full-root assessment stays
`blocked_missing_semantic_witnesses`.

Remaining work: compatible concrete base and full learned semantics; physical
refinement; goal/information witnesses; resource accounting/enforcement; full
operational recursive productivity; durable fresh-challenge/history consumption;
active atomic promotion/rollback; cross-turn operation and autonomous campaigns;
required independent and platform validation.
