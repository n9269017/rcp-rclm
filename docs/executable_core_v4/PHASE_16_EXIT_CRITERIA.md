# Phase 16 exit criteria

Phase 16 closes only when one exact source head satisfies every item below.

## Exact Phase 15 bootstrap

- Both retained Phase 15 archives match their frozen SHA-256 identities.
- The final Phase 15 closure, trajectory, bundle manifest, `M9` semantic package, and active store identities all match.
- The inherited multidomain planner manifest and int16 weights match.
- The fixed verifier and diagonal/classical semantic identifiers remain unchanged.

## Search and fairness

- Every turn exposes exactly the declared legal bounded mutation set.
- The inherited planner projection, novelty pressure, and rejection-conditioned ranking form only the bounded prefix.
- The fair enumerator is a complete permutation of the legal set.
- Candidate envelopes expand monotonically through the declared `4, 8, 16, 32` bounds as required.
- Every accepted promotion retains a fair-search certificate.
- Every campaign includes an independently checked complete bounded-exhaustion probe.

## Hidden challenge and promotion boundary

- Every challenge is generated after predecessor freeze.
- Target programs and target outputs are hidden from candidate search.
- The predecessor fails the fresh challenge and the accepted candidate passes it.
- Candidate self-reports are non-authoritative.
- The protected Phase 15 `M9` base package is never mutated.
- The entire predecessor capability frontier is retained at every promotion.
- The entire recursive-productivity frontier is retained at every promotion.
- At least one strict recursive-productivity expansion occurs; the authoritative campaigns demonstrate all eight family-specific additions.
- No successful host-authored transition schedule and no manual repair are present.

## Scaling ladder

For each of two fixed seeds and two fixed challenge streams:

- the 8-promotion campaign accepts;
- the 16-promotion campaign accepts;
- the 32-promotion campaign accepts; and
- the 64-promotion campaign accepts.

All campaigns must contain multiple rejection/recovery cycles, substantive update-family diversity, long-chain frontier retention, and complete exhaustion evidence. The 100-promotion campaign is retained as an optional stretch target rather than a closure prerequisite.
